Overview

A coworking space that moves.

Bigfoot Coworking needed content that could explain what they offer quickly, clearly, and in a way that felt alive on social media. The answer was two concurrent formats: a FAQ series that turned common questions into snappy, designed short-form videos, and an AI reel that used Google Flow to generate footage that no camera crew could shoot.

The AI reel wasn't about spectacle — it was about matching the ambition of the brand with visual storytelling that felt genuinely futuristic. The FAQ series, by contrast, was precise and human-paced: each episode 30–45 seconds, tight scripting, clear hierarchy.
