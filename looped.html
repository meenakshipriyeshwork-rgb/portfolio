Overview

Where your interests find each other.

Looped is a social community app designed around the idea that the best connections happen when people gather around a shared passion — not a profile. The app allows users to create and join micro-communities built on specific interests, from niche film genres to local running clubs to vintage fashion swaps.

The design system prioritises warmth over frictionless efficiency — it uses soft type, generous spacing, and an interface that rewards slow exploration. The goal was to build something that felt human-scaled: a place for communities, not content.
