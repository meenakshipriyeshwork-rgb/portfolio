"What if taking care of your gut felt less like a health routine and more like cracking open your favorite soda?"

Slingshot is a playful prebiotic drink designed for a generation that wants better choices without sacrificing fun. The challenge was to create a brand that makes gut health feel exciting, approachable, and refreshingly different from the overly serious wellness space. From vibrant packaging to energetic campaign visuals, every touchpoint was designed to spark curiosity, start conversations, and prove that feeling good can look just as good.

Packaging

The packaging was designed to stand out instantly on the shelf with a bold, playful aesthetic that reflects Slingshot's energetic personality. Bright colors, clean typography, and space-inspired illustrations come together to create a memorable identity while clearly communicating the product's key benefits. Every design element was crafted to make the can feel fun, modern, and as refreshing as the drink inside.

Overview

Built to make gut health feel anything but boring.

From naming the brand to launching it into the spotlight, Slingshot was built to make gut health feel anything but boring. The journey began with crafting a bold identity and playful nomenclature that captured the brand's energetic, rebellious spirit. That personality was then translated into vibrant packaging, where every color, illustration, and detail was designed to turn heads on the shelf.

The story didn't stop at the can. A complete launch ecosystem was created, including product mockups, campaign visuals, social media content, educational carousels, motion concepts, and launch creatives that introduced prebiotics in a fun, approachable way. Every touchpoint was designed to feel bold, witty, and unapologetically fresh — proving that taking care of your gut can be just as exciting as cracking open your favorite soda.
